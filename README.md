# Gen Z Festive Shopping Behaviour in Kolkata

**MBA Marketing Project | Marketing & Business Analytics | 2026**

## About the Project

What drives Gen Z consumers to choose one shopping channel over another during the festive season?

This research explores the shopping preferences, purchasing priorities and channel choices of 200 Gen Z consumers aged 18–26 in Kolkata and nearby towns.

## Research Objectives

- Understand the factors influencing festive shopping decisions.
- Compare preferences across physical stores, online marketplaces and quick commerce.
- Explore the role of digital platforms in consumer decisions.
- Identify consumer segments and develop marketing recommendations.

## Research Methodology

- Primary research using a Google Forms survey.
- 200 respondents aged 18–26.
- Excel-based data analysis using PivotTables and COUNTIFS.
- Correlation analysis and basic Python.
- Rule-based customer segmentation.

## Key Findings

- 72% prioritised price/value or product quality.
- Brand stores were the most-used individual shopping channel (28.5%).
- Quick Commerce and Online Marketplaces each accounted for 28%.
- Quick Commerce recorded the highest average spend (₹5,032).
- Instagram Reels was the leading reported digital influence (33%).

## Customer Segments

The study identified four consumer groups:

1. Explorers
2. Value Hunters
3. Brand Loyalists
4. Speed Seekers

## Marketing Recommendations

- Develop value bundles and targeted offers.
- Create engaging Instagram Reels campaigns.
- Strengthen in-store brand experiences.
- Use convenience-focused messaging for quick commerce shoppers.

## Skills Demonstrated

Consumer Research | Survey Design | Microsoft Excel | Data Analysis | Customer Segmentation | Marketing Strategy | Basic Python

## Project Limitations

The research uses a convenience sample, self-reported responses and data from one festive shopping period. The findings should not be generalised to all Gen Z consumers.
